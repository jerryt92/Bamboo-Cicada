## 中文

### 名称

### 宣传文本
把竹、纸、细绳做成的中华传统童玩装进 iPhone。摇一摇，听见竹知了的鸣声，也感到跟随节奏的震动反馈。

### 描述
竹知了是一款把中华传统民间童玩带到手机里的声音玩具。

传统竹知了以竹籤、细绳和纸筒做成，摇转时会发出近似鸣蝉的声响。这个 App 用手机的动作感应、声音与震动反馈，重现“轻轻摇动，听见夏天”的小小乐趣。

主要功能：
- 以竹林和卷轴风格呈现中华传统玩具
- 摇动手机，让竹知了随手势旋转
- 模拟鸣蝉声响，速度会跟随摇动节奏变化
- 通过震动反馈感受启动、旋转和节奏脉冲
- 中英文介绍竹知了的材料、玩法与民间童玩背景
- 适合亲子体验、传统文化展示、课堂引入和轻松解压
- 支持多语言界面

竹知了专注于一个简单体验：用手机重新遇见一种朴素、轻巧、带着夏天气息的中华传统声玩。

## English

### Name
Bamboo Cicada: Chinese Folk Sound Toy

### Subtitle
Shake gently, hear a small summer

### Promotional Text
Bring a traditional Chinese folk toy to iPhone. Shake to hear the bamboo cicada sing, with haptic feedback pulsing along with your hand rhythm.

### Description
Bamboo Cicada turns a traditional Chinese folk sound toy into a small iPhone experience.

The real bamboo cicada is usually made from bamboo, cord, and a paper cylinder. When swung by hand, it produces a sound like a cicada call. This app uses motion sensing, sound, and haptic feedback to recreate the gentle charm of shaking a summer toy in a bamboo forest.

Key features:
- A bamboo forest and scroll-inspired visual style
- Shake your iPhone to spin the bamboo cicada
- Cicada-like sound that changes with motion rhythm
- Haptic feedback for start pulses, spinning motion, and beat-like vibration
- Bilingual introduction to the toy's materials, play pattern, and folk background
- Useful for family play, cultural demos, classroom introductions, or a quiet moment of fun
- Multilingual interface support

Bamboo Cicada focuses on one simple feeling: meeting a humble traditional Chinese sound toy again through touch, motion, and the sound of summer.
